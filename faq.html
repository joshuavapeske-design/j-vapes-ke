Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our catalog authentication, instant delivery routes from Ruiru across Nairobi, age verification, and M-Pesa checkouts.

How do you verify age before sale?
In strict compliance with Kenyan statutory regulations, JVapes operates a rigid 18+ age verification policy. Automated age gates monitor platform access, and customer ID verification is required to confirm legal age. Logistics couriers will not hand over parcels to minors.
Do you deliver outside Nairobi?
Yes. JVapes coordinates prompt countrywide distribution across Kenya through reliable third-party courier parcel networks, ensuring secure shipments directly to your doorstep or local hub pickup points.
How fast is delivery?
Delivery timelines map according to clear destination parameters:
  • Ruiru, Nairobi & Surrounding Areas: Same-day or within-the-hour express boda delivery depending on your location coordinates and when your order is placed.
  • Outside Nairobi / Countrywide: Delivery windows typically vary between 24 to 48 hours depending on courier carrier schedules and transit distance.
Do you accept M-Pesa?
Yes. We prioritize modern mobile clearing pathways and accept direct automated payments through secure Lipa na M-Pesa transaction channels (Till Number: 1131829) along with WhatsApp and cash-on-delivery options.
Can I pay on delivery?
Pay on delivery is available for same-day delivery zones in Ruiru, Nairobi, and nearby locations. Remote nationwide parcel routes require clearing before courier transit.
Do you sell authentic products?
Yes, absolutely. JVapes operates a strict zero-counterfeit policy. We focus exclusively on supplying 100% verified authentic vape systems sourced directly from authorized manufacturers. Every disposable or starter kit box retains its independent scratch-off validation code.
Can I return a vape after opening it?
No. Due to strict public health, hygiene, and product safety protocols surrounding electronic nicotine platforms, all opened disposable vapes, pre-filled pods, e-liquids, heating coils, and standard consumables are entirely classified as Final Sale and cannot be returned once security seals are broken.
What happens if my device arrives defective?
If a hardware device exhibits a material factory flaw or arrives Dead on Arrival (DOA), you must report the issue to our technical team within 48 hours of physical receipt with original packaging for inspection and replacement.
How should I safely charge my rechargeable disposable vape?
Always charge your vape hardware using standard low-voltage outputs like a laptop port, gaming console, or a certified 5V/1A wall brick. Never attach vape systems to high-wattage fast phone chargers, which can damage the internal battery or scorch the coil.
