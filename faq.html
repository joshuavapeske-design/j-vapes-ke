How should I safely charge my rechargeable disposable vape or starter kit?
Always charge your vape hardware using standard low-voltage outputs like a laptop port, gaming console, or a certified **5V/1A wall brick**. Never attach vape systems to modern fast-charging blocks (such as high-wattage phone bricks). Doing so causes thermal stress, permanently damaging battery lifespans or burning out the internal mesh coil.
Why does my disposable vape taste burnt even though it is new?
This is typically caused by "chain vaping." The internal wicks require brief recovery windows to saturate fully with liquid after a draw. If you execute long, rapid back-to-back draws, the cotton will dry out and burn. We recommend waiting 15 to 30 seconds between draws to ensure consistent flavor preservation.
My pod device is leaking slightly. How do I fix this?
Minor condensation can pool near the magnet base over time. Simply snap the pod off, wipe the internal connection prongs cleanly with a paper towel, wrap a tissue over the mouthpiece, and shake the device downward firmly to clear any pooled fluid from the inner chimney line. Always avoid leaving devices exposed to high heat (e.g., inside a hot vehicle).
